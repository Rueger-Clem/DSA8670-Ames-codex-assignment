# Findings

Estimated relationships between lot area and sale price vary across neighborhoods. NridgHt has an estimated increase of $16.90 per additional square foot of lot area (n = 166; 95% confidence interval: $14.20–$19.60), compared with $4.81 in NAmes (n = 443; 95% confidence interval: $3.63–$6.00). Somerst and MeadowV also have positive estimated relationships. However, some confidence intervals include zero, including those for Blmngtn, NPkVill, and SawyerW, indicating uncertainty about whether their estimated slopes differ from zero. Blueste (n = 10), Greens (n = 8), GrnHill (n = 2), and Landmrk (n = 1) have fewer than 20 sales, so their estimates warrant particular caution. GrnHill’s slope should be interpreted descriptively, while Landmrk’s slope cannot be estimated from a single observation. The results table does not report a formal test comparing neighborhood slopes. These findings describe associations rather than causal effects and do not establish how accurately the models predict future sale prices.
